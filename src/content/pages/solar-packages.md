A solar package is a pre-designed system with matched panels, inverter, mounting and installation at a fixed price for typical homes or businesses. Every package includes standard installation, compliance paperwork, monitoring and the STC discount. Non-standard work, such as a switchboard upgrade, is quoted separately and shown in writing before you sign.

## What to compare between quotes

Put each quote side by side: system size, panel model, inverter model, warranty years (product and workmanship), electrical work included, monitoring and the final price after STCs.
