Panels produce the most around midday, but most homes use the most power after 3pm. A home battery stores your surplus solar so you use it in the evening instead of buying grid power at around 32c/kWh, rather than exporting it for 2c.

## How a battery works

During the day, solar runs your home first. Surplus solar charges the battery instead of being exported. After 3pm, the battery powers your home until it runs low, and the grid fills any gap. Each stored kWh is worth roughly 30c on Synergy's Home Plan (A1): the 32c you avoid buying, minus the 2c you no longer export.

## Who benefits most

- Homes that use a lot of power after 3pm: cooking, air conditioning, EV charging at night
- Solar systems that export a lot of surplus during the day
- Horizon Power customers, who receive a higher state rebate
- Anyone who wants backup during an outage

Read [is a solar battery worth it in Perth](/is-solar-battery-worth-it-perth/) for a simple payback method.

## What size do you need?

Many homes suit 10–14 kWh of usable capacity. The right size covers your evening and overnight use and still fills on most winter days. The WA rebate is capped at 10 kWh, and federal support per kWh is lower above 14 kWh. See [what size solar battery do I need](/what-size-solar-battery-wa/).

Already have solar? Most systems can take a battery using AC coupling or a hybrid inverter: see [adding a battery to existing solar](/add-battery-to-existing-solar-perth/).

## Backup options

A battery does not automatically mean backup. Backup needs an inverter or gateway that can run your home off-grid, wiring that separates the backed-up circuits, and a reserve kept in the battery. Most homes choose essential circuits (lights, fridge, internet, some power points). Read [will a solar battery keep my power on in a blackout](/solar-battery-blackout-backup-perth/).

## Rebates in brief

WA households can combine the WA Residential Battery Scheme rebate (up to $1,300 for Synergy customers or $3,800 for Horizon Power customers) with the federal Cheaper Home Batteries discount. The federal amount depends on your battery size and install date. Ask us for your exact figure. *Rebate settings checked October 2026.*

Eligible households can also borrow $2,001 to $10,000 interest-free. See [WA residential battery rebates](/solar-battery/wa-residential-battery-rebates/) for the details.

## Virtual Power Plants

To claim the WA rebate or loan, your battery must join an approved Virtual Power Plant (VPP), which can draw on part of your stored energy during a limited number of grid events. The federal discount needs a VPP-capable battery but not participation. Read [Virtual Power Plants in WA](/virtual-power-plant-wa/).
