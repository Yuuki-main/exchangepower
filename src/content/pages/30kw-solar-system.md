A 30kW commercial solar system produces about 130 kWh a day in Perth, or around 48,000 kWh a year. It suits offices, medical centres, small workshops and retail using roughly 150–300 kWh on a typical weekday. A system up to 30 kVA can usually connect under the standard connection rules introduced on 1 May 2026, which keeps approvals simple.

## What's included

- About 68 panels at 440 W on roughly 150–170 m² of roof
- A commercial inverter that meets AS/NZS 4777.2 and the current WA connection requirements
- Racking suited to your roof, including tilt frames for flat roofs
- Installation, switchboard connection and compliance paperwork
- Online monitoring with fault alerts

Brands we install include Sofar, Fronius, Jinko Solar, Neovolt, Solis, Growatt, Seraphim and Trina Solar. Your quote names the exact models, so you can check each one before you sign.

## Pricing and rebates

Systems up to 100kW are eligible for STCs, which reduce the price upfront. A 30kW system installed in 2026 creates about 207 STCs. The rest of the price depends on your roof, switchboard and access.

The return depends on how much solar you use on site. Read [how to calculate commercial solar ROI](/commercial-solar-roi-wa/) and compare [cash, finance and leasing](/commercial-solar-finance-options-wa/).

## Installation process

1. **Interval data review.** 12 months of bills or 30-minute interval data.
2. **Site assessment.** Roof, structure, switchboard and access.
3. **Design and proposal.** Output, savings and payback estimates.
4. **Installation.** Usually two to three days, with a short scheduled shutdown for the final connection.
5. **Commissioning and monitoring.** Testing, handover and fault alerts.
