We design and install solar for homes and businesses across Western Australia, from 6.6kW home systems to 100kW commercial systems. Every installation includes a site assessment, system design, approvals, installation, testing, commissioning, monitoring setup and handover documents.

## How our installation works

1. **Bill and roof review.** Send a recent bill and a photo of your switchboard. We look at your usage, roof direction, shade and supply.
2. **Design and quote.** A written quote with exact panel and inverter models, the STC discount, every inclusion and any switchboard work.
3. **Network and retailer paperwork.** We lodge the grid-connection application and set up DEBS with your retailer.
4. **Installation day.** Most homes take one day. Tile roofs, two-storey homes and larger systems may take two.
5. **Testing and commissioning.** We commission the system under the correct export arrangement for your retailer, as required since 1 May 2026.
6. **Handover and monitoring.** You receive the electrical compliance certificate, system diagram, warranty documents, monitoring login and shutdown instructions.

## Roofs we install on

Colorbond and metal sheeting, terracotta and concrete tile, and flat roofs with tilt frames. Asbestos roofs need specialist advice before any work. Roof direction, tilt and shade all change output; read [how roof direction and shading affect solar in Perth](/roof-orientation-shading-solar-perth/).

## The 2026 connection rules

Since 1 May 2026, systems up to 30 kVA can connect on a standard single- or three-phase connection, and new inverters must meet AS/NZS 4777.2 and support remote management (or be export-limited to 1.5 kW). See [single-phase vs three-phase solar in WA](/single-phase-vs-three-phase-solar-wa/).

## After installation

Solar needs little maintenance: an annual clean and an inspection every 2–3 years for homes, or every year for commercial systems. See our [cleaning and maintenance schedule](/solar-panel-cleaning-maintenance-perth/). If something goes wrong, call our local team on 08 6370 4963.
