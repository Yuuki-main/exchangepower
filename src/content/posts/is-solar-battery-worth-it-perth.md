A solar battery is worth it in Perth when it regularly replaces expensive evening grid power with your own midday solar, and when rebates bring the upfront cost down enough. With the WA state rebate and the federal discount, many homes can now reach payback within the battery's warranty. Here is how to check your own case. For an overview, see [home solar batteries in Perth](/solar-battery/).

## Where the saving comes from

Without a battery, surplus solar is exported at 2c/kWh before 3pm under DEBS, and you buy evening power at around 32c/kWh on Synergy's Home Plan (A1). A battery stores that surplus so you use it later. Each stored kWh is worth roughly 30c: the 32c you avoid buying, minus the 2c you no longer export. See [DEBS explained](/debs-synergy-solar-feed-in-explained/).

## A simple payback formula

**Payback (years) = (Battery price − WA rebate − Federal discount) ÷ (Daily kWh shifted × Value per kWh × 365)**

## Worked example (illustrative)

- 10 kWh usable battery, Synergy customer, 10kW solar.
- Battery cycles about 8.5 kWh on an average day across the year.
- Value per kWh about 30c, so about $2.55 a day, or roughly $930 a year.
- Any VPP credits come on top.

If the net price after the WA rebate ($1,300) and federal discount were $6,000, payback would be about 6.5 years before VPP credits. This is an example, not a promise: your result depends on your price, usage and tariff.

## Rebates that cut the upfront cost

| Support | Amount | Key condition |
| --- | --- | --- |
| WA Residential Battery Scheme (Synergy) | $130 per usable kWh, up to $1,300 | Approved product, SAA installer, VPP enrolment |
| WA Residential Battery Scheme (Horizon Power) | $380 per usable kWh, up to $3,800 | Same conditions |
| Federal Cheaper Home Batteries | Around 30% via STCs; tiered above 14 kWh; steps down every six months | Battery at least 5 kWh, VPP-capable |
| WA no-interest loan | $2,001–$10,000 over up to 10 years | Household income under $210,000, credit check |

See [WA residential battery rebates](/solar-battery/wa-residential-battery-rebates/) and the [no-interest loan guide](/wa-battery-scheme-no-interest-loan/).

## When a battery pays back fastest

- You use a lot of power after 3pm (cooking, air conditioning, EV charging at night).
- Your solar system exports a lot of surplus during the day, such as a [13.2kW system](/residential-solar/13-2kw-solar-system/).
- You are a Horizon Power customer, with a higher state rebate.
- You join a VPP that pays credits.
- You would value backup in an outage.

## When to wait or rethink

- Your solar system is small and exports little.
- Most of your use is already during the day.
- Your roof or switchboard needs costly upgrades first.
- You plan to sell the home within two or three years.

## Tariffs matter

Synergy's Midday Saver plan has very cheap daytime power and expensive 3pm–9pm power. With a battery covering the evening peak, Midday Saver can increase savings for some homes. Without a battery, it can raise bills for homes with high evening use. Ask for a comparison of both plans using your interval data.

## Battery life and warranty

Most home batteries carry about a 10-year warranty, with conditions on retained capacity or total energy throughput. Use the warranty period as a conservative life when judging payback.

## The verdict

For Perth homes with strong evening use and a decent solar system, a right-sized battery with rebates is often worth it. The best way to know is a quote based on your actual usage data. Start with [what size solar battery do I need](/what-size-solar-battery-wa/).
