Australia has a global leader on adoption of rooftop solar and homeowners around the country are also shifting towards solar energy as a way of saving on power bills and have more control on how much electricity their homes use. The 6.6kW solar system has become the most popular solar system among all the available residential solar systems. Combination of its low cost, capability to produce high energy, fit in regular Australian family housing, and high returns on investment has made it a perfect solution to a vast number of households in Australia.

The success of the **[6.6kW solar system](/residential-solar/6-6kw-solar-system-wa/)** is considerably contributed by its capability to provide sensible balance between size and price of the system. A significant number of Australian households have a high electricity usage level such that they would be able to use solar energy but the system will not have to be oversized. A 6.6kW solar system is placed right in the middle of the pack, and it is capable of giving their household enough to create enough power to meet their daily needs, and at the same time is affordable to the majority of home owners. Such a balance enables families to have significant savings without investing a lot of money in the first place.

Impressive energy output is one of the important factors that explain why homeowners opt to use 6.6kW solar system. In ideal conditions, a standard structure may produce up to about 24 to 29 kilowatt-hours of electricity daily depending on place, weather and direction of roof and efficiency of the panels used. This volume of production would suffice to meet the energy requirements of a large number of Australian households, especially those that have three to five people. The high production per day can decrease the dependence on grid power machine and decreases the monthly power billing.

The 6.6kW solar system is also successful due to climate in Australia. The bulk of areas enjoy incessant sunlight, which makes it ideal to generate solar power. States with high solar irradiance like Western Australia, Queensland, South Australia and New South Wales make residential systems efficient. A well set 6.6kW solar system is able to capitalize on conditions and produce unvarying energy throughout the year.

Another contributing factor to the popularity of 6.6kW solar system is its affordability. The last 10 years have seen a technology breakthrough in solar technology which has decreased the cost of installation significantly with government incentives. The present systems are very cost-effective and the homeowner can see his or her investment paid off in the form of electricity savings in a relatively short time. Solar is one of the most appealing household improvements as it has reduced total costs of installation and continuous cutbacks of the electricity bills.

One more consideration is the system design which is prevalent in Australia. Quite commonly a combination of 6.6kW of **[solar panels](/solar-packages/solar-panel-installation-in-wa/)** and a 5kW inverter is employed. This arrangement yields the optimum energy production in the morning and afternoon when the sun is not as powerful. Even though it may limit the peak production at certain times due to the inverter, the total energy produced tends to be enhanced during the day. This form of design is already a norm in the industry since it offers high level of performance and a high quality value to the homeowners.

The 6.6kW solar system is also very versatile. It is capable of comfortably serving the fundamental appliances used at home like refrigerators, laundry machines, televisions, lighting networks, air conditioners and kitchen appliances. The system provides sufficient electricity to power a big percentage of the family daytime energy expenditures to many families. The idle power may be sold to the electric power grid where it may yield homeowners feed-in credits to lower the total energy bills even more.

The increased interest in breaking energy dependence has also increased the demand of 6.6kW **[solar system](/residential-solar/)**. The increase in the electricity prices has stimulated the need by homeowners to have substitutes that offer more control of the costs of energy. With solar, the home can now produce electricity by themselves instead of always depending on electricity producers. This translates to protection against future rise in the price of energy and also allows the household to better balance their financial obligations in the long term.

The solar system of 6.6kW is extremely attractive since it is applicable to most forms of property. Installed on a suburban family house, a modern town and a country dwelling, the system can bring significant energy savings. The system has an appropriate size of about 28 to 35 square metres of roof space which most installations do not have. It is very popular with homeowners because of the flexibility of installation.

A 6.6kW Solar system can enable households to reduce their impact on the environment since it can produce clean electricity using sunlight. Each kilowatt-hour of solar panel energy will cut the number of fossil-produced electricity units. This will help make the environment cleaner and aid in the shift towards the renewable energy of Australia.
