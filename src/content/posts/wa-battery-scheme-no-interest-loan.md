The WA Residential Battery Scheme offers more than a rebate. Eligible households can also borrow up to $10,000 with no interest to help pay for a home battery. Here is who qualifies, what you need and how the process works. For the rebate amounts, see [WA residential battery rebates](/solar-battery/wa-residential-battery-rebates/).

## The loan at a glance

| Feature | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Loan amount | $2,001 to $10,000 |
| Interest | 0%, fixed for the whole term |
| Term | 3 to 10 years |
| Fees | No upfront or ongoing fees, according to the scheme administrator |
| Income test | Household gross annual income under $210,000 |
| Credit check | Yes, loans are subject to credit assessment and lending criteria |
| Administrator | Plenti, appointed by the WA Government |

The rebate itself is not income-tested. You can claim the rebate without the loan, or use both together.

## Who is eligible

You will generally need to:

- Be a Synergy or Horizon Power residential customer at the property.
- Install an approved battery from the supported product list.
- Use a vendor accredited with Plenti for the scheme, with an installer holding SAA Grid Connected Battery System accreditation.
- Enrol the battery in an approved Virtual Power Plant (see [Virtual Power Plants in WA](/virtual-power-plant-wa/)).
- Meet the income limit and pass a credit assessment for the loan.

The full criteria are on the WA Government's scheme page.

## Documents to have ready

1. Photo ID.
2. A recent electricity bill for the property.
3. Your most recent income tax return or notice of assessment, for income verification.
4. Details of all household members' income, if applicable.
5. Bank account details for repayments.

## Step by step

1. **Get a quote** from a Plenti-accredited vendor, with an approved battery.
2. **Rebate application** is created by the vendor in the Plenti portal.
3. **Apply for the loan** online with Plenti, choosing your amount and term.
4. **Approval** follows Plenti's credit and income checks.
5. **Installation** of the battery and VPP enrolment.
6. **Sign the loan** and confirm installation.
7. **Payment** of the rebate and loan funds goes to the vendor; you start repayments.

## Example repayments

| Loan | 5-year term | 10-year term |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $5,000 | About $83 a month | About $42 a month |
| $10,000 | About $167 a month | About $83 a month |

With no interest, repayments are simply the loan divided by the number of months. If your battery saves around $75–$80 a month, a longer term can make it close to cash-flow neutral from the start. See [is a solar battery worth it in Perth](/is-solar-battery-worth-it-perth/).

## Things to know before you borrow

- A credit check is part of the loan application.
- Missing repayments can affect your credit file, like any loan.
- Ask what happens to the loan if you sell the property.
- Make sure the battery is the right size first. Borrowing for a battery that never fills does not help. Read [what size solar battery do I need](/what-size-solar-battery-wa/).

## How long is the scheme open?

The scheme runs while funding is available. More than 37,000 rebates and 11,000 loans had been funded by October 2026, according to the administrator. Check current availability with your installer.
