If you have solar in Perth or the south-west, Synergy pays you for power you send back to the grid under the Distributed Energy Buyback Scheme (DEBS). The rate depends on the time of day, which changes how you should set up your system and your habits. For every incentive in one place, see [solar rebates and incentives in WA](/solar-packages/solar-rebates-and-incentives-in-wa/).

## What DEBS is

DEBS is the WA Government's buyback scheme for Synergy customers. It started on 31 August 2020 and replaced the older Renewable Energy Buyback Scheme (REBS) for new and upgraded systems. It pays the same rate for exports from solar and from batteries.

## Current rates

| Time of export | Buyback rate |
| --- | --- |
| 3pm to 9pm (peak) | 10c per kWh |
| Before 3pm and after 9pm (off-peak) | 2c per kWh |

Rates applied from 1 July 2025 and are reviewed by the State Government each year. DEBS pays on the first 50 kWh you export each day, which is more than most home systems send.

## Why the time of day matters

Most solar is exported in the middle of the day, when it earns only 2c. Meanwhile, power you buy from Synergy on the standard Home Plan (A1) costs around 32c/kWh. So:

- Every kWh of solar you use yourself is worth about 16 times more than a kWh exported at midday.
- West-facing panels shift some generation into the 3pm–9pm window, when exports earn 10c.
- A battery lets you store midday solar and use it in the evening, or export it at 10c during the peak.

## Who is eligible

You generally qualify for DEBS if you are a Synergy residential customer with an eligible new or upgraded solar or battery system, and your meter can record exports by time. Synergy confirms eligibility and any system-size limits during the connection process. A small one-off administration fee and metering costs may apply.

If you are still on REBS from an older system, upgrading or adding a battery usually moves you to DEBS. Compare both before you change anything.

## Horizon Power customers

Regional customers on Horizon Power have their own DEBS rates, which vary by town and are reviewed each July. Community Wave members may also access Horizon Power's Buyback Bonus. Check your current pricing schedule with Horizon Power.

## How to earn more from your solar under DEBS

1. **Use more of your own solar:** run appliances, pool pumps and hot water in the middle of the day.
2. **Consider west-facing panels:** if your evening use is high, some west orientation helps (see [roof direction and shading](/roof-orientation-shading-solar-perth/)).
3. **Add a battery:** store surplus and use it after 3pm instead of buying at full price. See [solar batteries](/solar-battery/).
4. **Join a VPP:** some programs pay extra for battery exports at peak times (see [Virtual Power Plants in WA](/virtual-power-plant-wa/)).
5. **Check your tariff:** Synergy's Midday Saver plan makes daytime grid power cheap and peak power expensive. It suits some households, especially with a battery.

## Does DEBS make solar less worth it?

No, but it changes where the value is. Solar pays back mainly through bill savings from self-use, not export income. Size your system to your daytime use, and treat exports as a small bonus.
