The federal solar rebate, paid as Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs), steps down every 1 January until it ends after 2030. If you are planning solar for your Perth home, a system installed in 2026 earns one more year of certificates than the same system installed in 2027. Here is what that difference is worth. For every incentive in one place, see [solar rebates and incentives in WA](/solar-packages/solar-rebates-and-incentives-in-wa/).

## How the STC discount works

When you install an eligible system up to 100kW, it can create STCs. Most homeowners assign them to the installer, who takes their value off your price upfront. The number of STCs is:

**STCs = system size (kW) × zone rating × deeming years**

Perth is in Zone 3, with a rating of 1.382. The deeming period is 5 years for 2026 installations and 4 years for 2027, reducing by one each year until the scheme ends.

## What changes for Perth systems

| System size | STCs if installed in 2026 | STCs if installed in 2027 | Fewer certificates | Approx. value lost at $38/STC |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [6.6kW](/residential-solar/6-6kw-solar-system-wa/) | 45 | 36 | 9 | About $340 |
| [7.7kW](/residential-solar/7-7kw-solar-system-wa/) | 53 | 42 | 11 | About $420 |
| [10kW](/residential-solar/10kw-solar-system-wa/) | 69 | 55 | 14 | About $530 |
| [13.2kW](/residential-solar/13-2kw-solar-system/) | 91 | 72 | 19 | About $720 |

STC prices move with the market. The $38 figure is illustrative; your quote will use the current price.

## Is it worth rushing?

A saving of $340–$720 is real, but it is not a reason to accept a poor design or a cheap installer. Two things matter more:

1. **Install date, not signing date, sets the deeming period.** A quote signed in December that is installed in January gets the 2027 rate. Ask your installer to confirm an installation date in writing.
2. **Every month without solar is a month of full-price power.** For a typical 6.6kW system saving around $110 a month, waiting three months costs about as much as the STC drop. Bring forward your decision, not your standards.

## Batteries change on a different schedule

The federal Cheaper Home Batteries discount also uses STCs, but with a separate factor that steps down every six months, including on 1 January. Since 1 May 2026 it is also tiered by battery size, with lower support per kWh above 14 kWh. The WA Residential Battery Scheme rebate (up to $1,300 for Synergy customers or $3,800 for Horizon Power customers) is a fixed amount and does not step down, but it is limited by the scheme budget. See [WA residential battery rebates](/solar-battery/wa-residential-battery-rebates/).

If you are installing solar and a battery together, the January step-down affects both discounts at once.

## Busy-season tip

November and December are the busiest months for Perth installers. If you want a 2026 installation date, get your site assessment done early and keep switchboard photos, a recent bill and roof access ready.

## What to check on your quote

- The number of STCs and the price per STC used.
- The planned installation date and what happens if it slips into January.
- Panel and inverter models, and May 2026 connection compliance.
- Any battery discount shown separately from the solar STC discount.

## The bottom line

Installing before 1 January 2027 keeps one extra year of STCs. Plan early, choose a quality system, and make sure the installation date is confirmed.
