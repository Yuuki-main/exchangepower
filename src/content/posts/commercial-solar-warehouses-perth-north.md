Perth's northern industrial areas, including Wangara, Malaga, Osborne Park, Balcatta and Landsdale, are full of large, flat-pitched metal roofs sitting in full sun. Those roofs are some of the best solar real estate in the city. Exchange Power is based in Wangara (see [solar panel installation in Wangara](/solar-packages/solar-panel-installation-wangara/)), so these are the sites we know best.

## Why industrial roofs suit solar

- **Large, unshaded roof areas** that fit a [50kW](/commercial-solar/50kw-solar-system/) to [100kW](/commercial-solar/100kw-solar-system/) system without complex layouts.
- **Metal sheeting** (Kliplok, Trimdek and similar) that takes clamp or rail mounting quickly.
- **Heavy daytime loads** from compressors, refrigeration, lighting, air handling and forklift charging.
- **Weekday operating hours** that line up well with solar generation.

## Typical loads and solar fit

| Site type | Main loads | Solar fit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cold storage and food distribution | Refrigeration 24/7, peaks in afternoon heat | Excellent; consider a battery for evenings |
| Fabrication and engineering workshops | Welders, compressors, CNC, lighting | Very good on weekdays |
| Trade supply and wholesale | Lighting, air conditioning, forklift charging | Good to very good |
| Automotive and panel beaters | Compressors, spray booths, hoists | Very good |
| Showrooms and offices | Lighting, air conditioning, IT | Good |

## Checks before we design

1. **Roof structure:** older purlins may need an engineer's assessment for added load and wind uplift.
2. **Roof condition:** rust, fixings and sealants; it is far cheaper to repair before installation.
3. **Asbestos:** pre-1990 buildings may contain asbestos roofing, which needs specialist advice before any work.
4. **Safe access:** edge protection, roof anchors or walkways for installation and future maintenance.
5. **Switchboard and supply:** main switchboard capacity, three-phase supply and space for protection.
6. **Network application:** systems above 30 kVA need a Western Power application.

## Owners, tenants and leases

Many industrial units are leased. Solar can still work:

- **Owner-occupier:** the simplest case; you pay and you save.
- **Landlord installs:** may increase rent or charge tenants for solar power under an agreement.
- **Tenant installs:** needs landlord consent and a clear agreement on removal or transfer at lease end.

Agree in writing who owns, maintains and insures the system.

## Installing without stopping work

Most commercial installs are planned so the business keeps running. Panels and racking go on the roof during normal hours. The final switchboard connection usually needs a short, scheduled shutdown, which can be booked early in the morning or on a weekend.

Commercial systems should have monitoring that alerts you to faults. Dust from industrial activity and nearby roads builds up faster than on homes, so cleaning and inspections are worth scheduling. See our [cleaning and maintenance schedule](/solar-panel-cleaning-maintenance-perth/).
